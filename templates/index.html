Music App

A database-driven Full Stack Music Discovery and Playlist Management Application.

Current Project Progress:

  • Authentication & User Roles: Completed. Secure registration, login, logout, and server-side role enforcement (ADMIN vs. MEMBER).
  • Database Architecture: Completed and normalized (1NF, 2NF, 3NF) with 9 application tables and database-level constraints.
  • Music Catalogue: Browse, search, and filter songs by artist and genre.
  • Personal Collections: Save favourites and curate private playlists.
  • Playback: Play credited sample tracks in the shared player.
  • Project Status: Phases 1–12 are complete; final quality assurance is underway.

Configured Applications:

  • accounts: User authentication, registration, login/logout, profile management, and role-based permissions.
  • catalog: Artists, songs, genres, catalogue management, and search.
  • playlists: Personal playlist curation, management, and track ordering.
  • profiles: User profile information and favourite song bookmarks.
